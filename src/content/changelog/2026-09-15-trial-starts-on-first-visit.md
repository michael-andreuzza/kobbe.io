---
title: Trials start when Kobbe sees your first visit
date: 2026-09-15
description: Cardless trials no longer count down from signup. Your 15 days begin when the tracker records its first pageview, with a guided setup flow on the home page.
---

If you signed up but had not installed the script yet, the old trial clock was already ticking. That is gone for cardless trials: your 15 days start when Kobbe records the first pageview on your site, not when you create the account.

**New home page setup.**

While you are waiting for that first visit, the home page walks you through three steps: add your site, copy the snippet, and listen for data. Platform install guides live on kobbe.io; the onboarding screen links straight to them, and the same picker is in **Settings → Installation** once you have a site.

**Trial status in the sidebar.**

Active trials show days left in a compact card above the events meter. The home page keeps a single quiet line with a **Choose a plan** button until the last three days, when the full notice returns.
