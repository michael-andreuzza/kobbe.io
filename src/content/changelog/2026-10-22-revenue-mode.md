Pages, sources, and locations have always ranked by visitors. With revenue connected, you can now flip the whole dashboard to rank by money instead.

**One click.**

Select the Revenue tile at the top. Every breakdown panel re-sorts by revenue, and the bars size by revenue too. Click another tile to go back to visitors.

**Visitors stay in view.**

Each row still shows its visitor count, so a page with few visitors and high revenue stands out next to one with lots of traffic and no sales.

Revenue mode needs a connected payment provider. See [revenue attribution](/docs/revenue-attribution) to set one up.
