Four in one release. Segment filters slice every dashboard view by source, location, device, and more. Chart notes let you pin annotations to dates ("launched on HN") so spikes explain themselves later. A weekly digest email summarizes each site's week. And [404 tracking](/docs/404-tracking) surfaces broken links, with the page visitors came from so you can fix the source.
