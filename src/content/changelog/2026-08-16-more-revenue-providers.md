date: 2026-08-16
description: Four more payment providers can now feed revenue attribution, alongside Stripe, Polar, Paddle, and the rest.
---

Revenue attribution now accepts webhooks from Whop, RevenueCat, Superwall, and Shopify, joining Stripe, Polar, Paddle, Creem, Mollie, and Revolut. Point the provider's webhook at Kobbe and purchases start attributing to the pages and sources that drove them. Setup guides for every provider are in [Revenue attribution](/docs/revenue-attribution).
