---
title: Events explorer and insights
date: 2026-07-22
description: A dedicated Events page with a filterable activity log, plus an Insights view over your custom events.
---

Custom events graduated from a card to a page: the new Events explorer shows a live, filterable [activity log](/docs/events-activity-log) of everything your sites send, and the Insights view aggregates events over time so you can see which ones are growing. Realtime got a shared dashboard treatment in the same release.
