---
title: Saved views
date: 2026-10-22
description: Save a set of dashboard filters and get back to it in one click.
---

If you keep rebuilding the same filters, like Google visitors on mobile or traffic from one country, you can now save them.

**Save the filters you use most.**

Filter the dashboard, then choose **Save view** next to your filters. Give it a name and it shows up in the **Views** menu for everyone in the workspace.

**Works with any date range.**

A view stores filters, not dates. Open it this week or last quarter and it applies the same filters to whatever range you have picked.

See [Saved views](/docs/dashboard-overview#saved-views) for the details.
