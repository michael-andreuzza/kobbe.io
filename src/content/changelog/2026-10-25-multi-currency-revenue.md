---
title: Revenue in every currency you sell in
date: 2026-10-25
description: Sites that sell in more than one currency now get full revenue reports, one currency at a time, with no conversion.
---

Selling in more than one currency no longer hides your numbers.

**Currency picker on the Revenue page.**

When a range has sales in more than one currency, pick the currency in the toolbar. Totals, attribution, breakdowns, products, and refunds all follow it. Kobbe starts with the currency that earned the most, and the choice is saved in the URL. [Details](/docs/revenue#multi-currency)

**Real numbers on the overview.**

The revenue KPI, the chart overlay, and breakdown rows now show your main currency, labeled for example **Net in USD**, instead of a "Multiple currencies" notice. Shared dashboards work the same way.

**Exact amounts, any currency.**

Every sale stays in the currency your provider charged, so amounts always match your provider. Currencies without cents, like JPY and KRW, now display correctly from every provider.
