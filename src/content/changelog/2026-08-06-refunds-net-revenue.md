---
title: Refund tracking and net revenue
date: 2026-08-06
description: Refunds now flow into the Revenue page, with net revenue KPIs and a recent refunds card.
---

Revenue used to be gross-only, which flattered the numbers. Refunds are now tracked from provider webhooks: the [Revenue page](/docs/revenue) shows net revenue KPIs alongside gross, plus a recent refunds card so you see what came back and from which product.
