---
title: Password-protected shared dashboards
date: 2026-10-22
description: Add a password to a public dashboard link, so only the people you give it to can open it.
---

A shared link is handy for client reports, but anyone who gets hold of the URL can open it.

**Add a password to any shared dashboard.**

Go to **Settings → Sharing**, and under **Password**, set one with 6 to 128 characters. Viewers see a short password screen first, and their browser stays unlocked for 30 days.

**Change or remove it anytime.**

Changing the password locks out everyone who used the old one. Removing it makes the link open again. Regenerating the link keeps the password.
