The dotted sphere on the realtime page is gone. In its place is a proper map, built on open map data and styled to match the rest of the dashboard.

**Flat map or globe, your call.**

A new button in the map controls switches between a flat world map and a 3D globe. Your choice is remembered per browser. The globe rotates slowly on its own and pauses while you interact with it.

**Same privacy.**

Nothing about the data changed. Dots are placed from coarse, edge-derived location signals; Kobbe still does not store raw IP addresses.
