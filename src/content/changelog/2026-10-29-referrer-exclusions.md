---
title: Block spam referrers from your dashboard
date: 2026-10-29
description: Exclusions now take a referring site, so you can drop fake referral traffic that Kobbe's spam list hasn't caught yet.
---

Spam referrers no longer have to sit in your sources.

**Referrer rules.**

In **Settings → Exclusions**, pick **Referrer** and enter a domain. New visits from that site and its subdomains are dropped before they count, within about a minute. [Details](/docs/exclude-visits#referrer-rules)

**Built-in spam list, now with Kobbe's own additions.**

Kobbe already drops 2,300+ known spam referrers for every site. Spoofed referrers we spot on Kobbe sites are now added on top, so everyone gets the fix.
