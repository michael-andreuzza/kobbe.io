---
title: Dashboards got fast
date: 2026-06-16
description: Daily rollups and cached views make range switching feel instant, even on all-time windows.
---

A performance push across the whole dashboard: traffic stats are pre-aggregated into daily rollups, breakdown responses are cached, and switching date ranges reuses warm data instead of recomputing. Long windows that used to take seconds, including all-time, now render close to instantly.
