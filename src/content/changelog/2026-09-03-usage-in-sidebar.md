---
title: Events usage in the sidebar
date: 2026-09-03
description: A compact meter above the site switcher shows how much of this month's event allowance you've used, on desktop and mobile.
---

You no longer need to open Settings to see where you stand on events. A small card above the site switcher shows the month's count and percentage, turns amber past 80%, and red at the cap. On paid plans the red state says outright that collection is paused, so you're never guessing why a chart went flat. Tap the card to jump to **Settings → Usage**, where you can also switch it off if you'd rather not see it.
