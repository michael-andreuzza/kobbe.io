title: Revenue per visitor on every row
date: 2026-10-22
description: In revenue mode, each page, source, and location now shows how much it earns per visitor, next to its revenue.
---

Raw revenue favours busy pages. A page with lots of traffic can top the list while a quieter page turns far more of its visitors into customers.

**Earnings per visitor, row by row.**

Click the Revenue tile, and every row that earned something now shows its revenue per visitor next to its revenue, for example `$7.27/visitor`. It works across pages, sources, channels, locations, devices, and campaigns.

**Nothing new is tracked.**

It is calculated from the revenue and visitor counts the dashboard already has, so no extra data is collected.
