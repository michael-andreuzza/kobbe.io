Search Console tells you where you rank. It does not tell you what to fix first.

**A shortlist of queries worth your time.**

Open the **Opportunities** tab on the Google search keywords card, and Kobbe flags three kinds of queries:

- **Almost page one**: queries ranking around positions 4 to 20 with real impressions, the easiest ones to push up.
- **Few clicks**: queries where you already rank in the top five, but searchers rarely click. A clearer title and description usually helps.
- **Pages compete**: queries where two or more of your pages rank and split the clicks between them.

Expand the card to see every opportunity with a suggested fix.

**Nothing new is tracked.**

It is calculated from the Search Console data Kobbe already syncs, with no new Google permissions.
