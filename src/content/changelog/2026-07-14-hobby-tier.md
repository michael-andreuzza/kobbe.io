---
title: Hobby tier
date: 2026-07-14
description: A smaller, cheaper entry tier for side projects, with every feature included.
---

Not every site needs hundreds of thousands of events. The new Hobby tier gives side projects a cheaper way in, with the same rule as every other tier: all features included, you only pick the volume. Current tiers and limits are in [Billing and usage](/docs/billing-and-usage-limits).
