---
title: Page groups
date: 2026-10-22
description: Combine pages into sections like Blog or Docs and see unique visitors per section.
---

Individual paths are great until you have hundreds of them and want to know how the blog is doing as a whole.

**Group pages by path.**

The Pages card has a new **Groups** tab. Create a group with a name and a path like `/blog/*`, which covers `/blog` and everything under it. Use `*` anywhere as a wildcard.

**Visitors counted once per section.**

Someone who reads five blog posts counts once for Blog. When a page fits several groups, it counts in the most specific one, so `/blog/guides/*` wins over `/blog/*`. Groups follow your date range and filters.

See [Page groups](/docs/dashboard-stats-pages#page-groups) for the details.
