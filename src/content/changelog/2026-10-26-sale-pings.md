Know where a sale came from the moment it happens.

**A message for every sale.**

Paste a Slack or Discord webhook in **Site settings → Reports & alerts**, and each new paid sale posts to your channel with the amount, the product, the source, the landing page, and the last page before checkout. [Setup guide](/docs/sale-pings)

**No personal data.**

Pings carry the same aggregate attribution you see in Kobbe. No names, emails, or buyer details ever leave your payment provider.

**Clearer revenue dropdowns.**

The lookback and attribution model pickers on the Revenue page now show readable labels when closed.
