Mark any page visit or custom event as a conversion and Kobbe tracks the rate everywhere: which sources convert, which campaigns pay off, which landing pages actually do the job. First-party collection shipped in the same release, letting the tracker run from your own domain. Details in [Conversions](/docs/conversions).
