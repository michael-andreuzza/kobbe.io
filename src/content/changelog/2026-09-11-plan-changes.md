---
title: Upgrade or downgrade your plan anytime
date: 2026-09-11
description: Subscribers can now move between event volume tiers from billing settings, with proration handled automatically.
---

If you're on a paid plan and your traffic grows past your tier, or drops below it, you can now change plans directly from billing settings.

Open Settings, then Billing, and choose "Change plan". You'll land in the customer portal where you can switch to any tier, up or down.

The price difference is prorated automatically, so you only ever pay for what you actually use. No emails, no waiting on support.
