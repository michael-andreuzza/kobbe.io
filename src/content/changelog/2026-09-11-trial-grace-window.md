---
title: Your visitors keep counting after your trial ends
date: 2026-09-11
description: Kobbe now keeps recording traffic for 14 days after a trial or subscription lapses, so subscribing later doesn't leave a gap in your data.
---

Until now, data collection stopped the moment a trial ran out. If you subscribed a week later, that week was simply missing from your charts.

That's fixed. When a trial or subscription lapses, Kobbe keeps counting your visitors for 14 more days. The dashboard stays locked until you pick a plan, but the data underneath never stops.

So if you need a few days to decide, you won't come back to a hole in your analytics. Subscribe and everything collected in the meantime is already there.
