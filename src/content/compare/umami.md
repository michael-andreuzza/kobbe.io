## Where Umami is the better pick

- You want to self-host, or open source matters to you.
- You want to choose between US and EU data storage.
- You want session replays and heatmaps in the same tool.

## Where Kobbe is the better pick

- You want revenue from Stripe, Polar, Paddle, or Shopify tied to each page, without writing revenue events.
- You want Search Console data next to your traffic.
- You want AI agents that can also set up goals and funnels, not just read your stats.

## Switching from Umami

Import your Umami history into Kobbe with an API key, from Umami Cloud or a self-hosted instance, or from a ZIP export. Kobbe only fills the days before your first Kobbe pageview, so nothing gets overwritten. See [Import from Umami](/docs/import-from-umami).
