## Where DataFast is the better pick

- You want subscription metrics like MRR, LTV, and churn in your analytics.
- You want return on ad spend for Meta Ads.
- You need a React Native SDK.

## Where Kobbe is the better pick

- You want analytics without cookies or a consent banner, out of the box.
- You want bots, datacenter traffic, and referrer spam filtered for you.
- You want Web Vitals next to your traffic and revenue.

## Switching from DataFast

Import your DataFast history into Kobbe with a read-only website API key. Kobbe only fills the days before your first Kobbe pageview, so nothing gets overwritten. See [Import from DataFast](/docs/import-from-datafast).
