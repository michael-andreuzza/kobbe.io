## Where Plausible is the better pick

- You want to self-host, or open source matters to you.
- You need your data stored on EU-owned servers.
- You want a tool that has been running since 2018.

## Where Kobbe is the better pick

- You want revenue from Stripe, Polar, Paddle, or Shopify tied to each page, without writing revenue events.
- You want Web Vitals, funnels, and Search Console in one dashboard.
- You want AI agents that can read your analytics and set up goals and funnels for you.

## Switching from Plausible

Import your Plausible history into Kobbe with a Stats API key or a full site export. Kobbe only fills the days before your first Kobbe pageview, so nothing gets overwritten. See [Import from Plausible](/docs/import-from-plausible).
