## Where Fathom is the better pick

- You want your data kept for as long as you stay a customer.
- You want visitor data processed in the EU before it is stored.
- You want a smaller script.

## Where Kobbe is the better pick

- You want revenue from Stripe, Polar, Paddle, or Shopify tied to each page, without writing revenue events.
- You want funnels and Web Vitals next to your traffic.
- You want AI agents that can read your analytics and set up goals and funnels for you.

## Switching from Fathom

Import your Fathom history into Kobbe from a daily ZIP export. Kobbe only fills the days before your first Kobbe pageview, so nothing gets overwritten. See [Import from Fathom](/docs/import-from-fathom).
