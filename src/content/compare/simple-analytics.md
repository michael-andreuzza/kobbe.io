## Where Simple Analytics is the better pick

- You need your data stored on EU-owned servers.
- You want a built-in AI chat inside the dashboard.
- You want a lighter script option.

## Where Kobbe is the better pick

- You want revenue from Stripe, Polar, Paddle, or Shopify tied to each page, without writing revenue events.
- You want Web Vitals and Search Console in the same dashboard as your traffic.
- You want AI agents that can read your analytics and set up goals and funnels for you.

## Switching from Simple Analytics

Add the Kobbe script next to Simple Analytics and run both for a few weeks. Once you trust the numbers, remove Simple Analytics. See [Installation guides](/docs/installation-guides).

Import your Simple Analytics history straight from its Stats API. Kobbe only fills the days before your first Kobbe pageview, so nothing gets overwritten. See [Import from Simple Analytics](/docs/import-from-simple-analytics).
