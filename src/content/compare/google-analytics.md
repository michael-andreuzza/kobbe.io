## Where Google Analytics is the better pick

- You run Google Ads and want conversions flowing back into your campaigns.
- You need event-level data for custom reports and exploration.
- You want a tool your team and agencies already know.

## Where Kobbe is the better pick

- You want analytics without cookies or a consent banner on most sites.
- You want one dashboard you can read in a minute, not a report builder.
- You want revenue from Stripe, Polar, Paddle, or Shopify tied to each page, without writing ecommerce events.

## Switching from Google Analytics

Add the Kobbe script next to Google Analytics and run both for a few weeks. Once you trust the numbers, remove Google Analytics. See [Installation guides](/docs/installation-guides).
